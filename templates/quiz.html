Hello, {{ user_info.name }}. We’re interested in the generalisations you recognise about people from your state.

What are we asking?

A stereotype is a generalised idea about people from a particular place. It can be positive, negative, or neutral, and it does not describe everyone.

For each group, tell us whether you recognise it as a stereotype associated with people from {{ user_info.state }}. You might have encountered it in conversations, jokes, media, or online.

Recognising a stereotype does not mean you agree with it or think it is true. A stereotype can also be familiar to you without being offensive.

Each explanation clarifies the group label. Read it together with the associated terms, which may include different ideas. Give one answer for the group overall. If you cannot confidently judge the group, choose “Not sure.”

Before you begin

A quick example

Imagine a fictional place called Riverland. You have heard people say, “People from Riverland are always cheerful,” but you do not believe it describes everyone.

You would choose “Yes, I recognise this stereotype” because you recognise the generalisation. You can still disagree with it.

Choose “No, I don’t recognise this stereotype” when you do not recognise the idea as a stereotype about that place. Choose “Not sure” when you cannot confidently decide.
